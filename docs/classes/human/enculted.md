# Enculted

**Fantasy:** tapped into something ancient living in the network. The augmentations that make the Cyborg powerful are blocking signals the unmodified mind can receive. Unstable, dangerous power with a horror edge.

**Attribute:** Ambition — the more corrupt, the more powerful. Opposes Clarity. See [Attribute System](../../design/attribute-system.md).

**Tier perks:** Stacking Ambition unlocks curse abilities (Tier 1 curse at 40%). See [Stat Identity](../../design/attribute-system.md#stat-identity--tier-perks--visual-metamorphosis).
