# Analog

Unaugmented. In a world that sells upgrades on every corner, the Analog refused — or never had the choice. Their power comes from what the Cyborg cut away.

**Tier perks:** Unlocked through talent tree progression. Deeper investment transforms the character — high-tier Analog perks feel grotesque and fleshy. The origin class rewards breadth across its three specializations; specialists reward depth.
