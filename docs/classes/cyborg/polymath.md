# Polymath *(ranged)*

**Fantasy:** augment the mind beyond human limits. The body is just a platform for expanded cognition.

**Attribute:** Clarity — mental precision and cognitive reach. Opposes Ambition. See [Attribute System](../../design/attribute-system.md).

**Tier perks — Telekinesis.** Every 6 seconds, **1 / 2 / 4** thin psionic beams emit from the player's head, each anchoring on a distinct nearby enemy. Over 2 seconds the target is lifted into the air, then slammed back to the ground for direct + radial AoE damage. Bolts within the same trigger pick distinct targets when possible.
