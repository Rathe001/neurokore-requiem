# Lighting

## Design Philosophy

Darkness is the default state of the world. Well-lit areas are rare and should feel like relief — not a mechanical reward, but a psychological one. The player learns to associate light with safety, rest, and human presence. That conditioning is built slowly and paid off deliberately.

Light sources carry no mechanical advantage over one another. The choice of light source is personal — a style decision that shapes how a playthrough looks and feels without affecting what the player can do.

---

## The World Is Dark

The darkness is not an artistic choice — it is the result of corporate withdrawal. Earth Facility #723 and the zones beyond it run on infrastructure that was abandoned when it stopped being profitable. Power grids failed or were cut. Emergency lighting ran out. Whatever remains is incidental.

Darkness is not explained to the player. It simply is. The world looks like this now.

**Well-lit areas signal one of three things:**
- Someone is still here and wants to be found
- Someone is still here and doesn't care if they're found
- Something is drawing power that shouldn't be

## Light Sources

Light sources are equippable gear occupying a dedicated **utility slot** — separate from power gear so they never compete with build choices. Any light source can be equipped by any spec. Starting light sources are thematic defaults, not locked assignments.

### Properties

Each light source has three properties:

| Property | Description |
|---|---|
| **Beam type** | Directional (cone), wide (shallow cone), or omnidirectional |
| **Color** | The tint of the light cast into the environment |
| **Reliability** | Steady, intermittent (occasional flicker), or unstable (frequent failure) |

None of these properties confer mechanical advantage. They affect how the environment looks and how the playthrough feels.

### Starting Light Sources by Spec

Each spec begins with a thematic default that can be replaced through loot, purchase, or crafting.

| Spec | Default Light Source | Beam Type | Color | Reliability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Forged** | Chest-mounted industrial lamp | Wide forward | Amber / orange | Steady |
| **Automaton** | Scout drone overhead light | Omnidirectional | Cold blue-white | Steady |
| **Polymath** | Scanning ocular implant | Sweeping directional | Violet | Intermittent |
| **Survivalist** | Jury-rigged handheld torch | Directional | Warm yellow | Unstable |
| **Gentleman / Lady** | Precision monocle lamp | Narrow directional | Crisp white | Steady |
| **Enculted** | *See below* | Directional | Sickly green | Intermittent |
| **Base Class** | Standard flashlight | Directional | White | Steady |

### The Enculted Light

The Enculted's default light source behaves like the others with one exception: it occasionally illuminates things that are not there.

This is not announced. It is not explained. Players who notice it will draw their own conclusions.

The same effect exists as a rare loot item — **Corrupted Light Source** — equippable by any spec. A player running the Forged who finds one and equips it is making a statement about their playthrough.

### Found Light Sources

Light sources drop as loot, appear in vendor inventories, and can be crafted. Properties vary across the full range. Notable types:

- **Flare** — single-use, wide omnidirectional, bright, burns out
- **Bioluminescent Vial** — unstable omnidirectional, organic green glow, origin unclear
- **Corp Emergency Lantern** — wide steady white, clearly abandoned equipment
- **Stripped Drone Lamp** — omnidirectional, cold white, salvaged Automaton hardware
- **Corrupted Light Source** — rare, sickly green, intermittent, illuminates things that are not there

## Generators

Scattered throughout dark zones, generators can be activated to permanently illuminate a room or section of a level for the duration of that run. They do not respawn between runs.

Finding and activating a generator is not a puzzle. It is a small moment of agency — the player chose to push into the dark, found the generator, and the room came back to life. That's enough.

## Zone Lighting Tiers

| Tier | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Blackout** | No ambient light. Player light source only. | Sub-Level Zero, deep abandoned zones |
| **Failing** | Intermittent flickering institutional lighting. Unreliable. | Earth Facility #723 starting zones |
| **Dim** | Low ambient light. Functional but oppressive. | Most outdoor and transitional zones |
| **Lit** | Functioning lights, human presence implied. | Safe houses, vendor hubs, key story locations |
| **Relief** | Intentional dramatic lighting — a doorway, a room, a beacon. | End of Sub-Level Zero, key payoff moments |

The player will spend most of the game in Blackout and Failing tiers. Lit and Relief tiers should be used sparingly and deliberately.

---

## Starting Zone Lighting

Both starting zones begin in the **Failing** tier — institutional lighting that flickers, dims, and fails in rolling waves. The Human path begins mid-blackout (the inciting power cut). The Cyborg path wakes in a ward where half the lights have already failed.

Neither starting zone reaches full Blackout — there is always *some* light, however unreliable. This eases the player into the lighting system before Sub-Level Zero removes that comfort entirely.
