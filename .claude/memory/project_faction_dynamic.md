---
name: Analog vs Cyborg faction dynamic
description: The two origin paths oppose each other ideologically but unite against the corporation as a shared enemy
type: project
---

Analog and Cyborg tracks are ideologically opposed but united against the greater threat — the monopoly corporation that owns everything and values fractional profit gains over life or freedom.

**Why:** The player should feel factional tension without factional war. The corporation is the true antagonist. Neither origin likes the other, but "the enemy of my enemy is my friend" — however temporary that alliance might be.

**How to apply:** NPCs, dialog, safe houses, and story beats should reflect uneasy cooperation, not open warfare between origins. The corporation should always feel like the bigger problem. Don't erase the distrust — lean into it — but always frame it as secondary to survival against corporate indifference.
